`KNOWN EXPLOITED` `CRITICAL` `CVSS 9.8` `published 2025-09-05`

> **KNOWN EXPLOITED.** CISA lists this as known-exploited in Linux Kernel on 2026-09-18, so it is being used against people now -- that outranks severity.

**What happened:** A critical flaw in the Linux kernel TLS implementation mishandles zero-length records on the receive list, potentially causing processing errors during recvmsg calls. **How serious:** This vulnerability is rated Critical with a CVSS score of 9.8, indicating it can lead to complete compromise of confidentiality, integrity, and availability.
