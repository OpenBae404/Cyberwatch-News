3. KNOWN EXPLOITED -- CVE-2026-102490

KNOWN EXPLOITED CRITICAL CVSS 9.4 published 2026-09-30

KNOWN EXPLOITED. CISA lists this as known-exploited in Zammad GmbH Zammad on 2026-10-02, so it is being used against people now -- that outranks severity.

What happened: Zammad packages built with packager.io contained a privilege escalation flaw where service processes briefly ran as root and executed files writable by the unprivileged zammad account. An attacker with local write access to the zammad service account could automatically escalate to full root privileges without administrator interaction.

Who is affected: Administrators running Zammad installations from DEB or RPM packages are affected, while source installations and official container images are not.

How serious: The vulnerability is rated Critical with a CVSS score of 9.4, allowing complete system compromise due to automatic service restarts facilitating rapid privilege escalation.

What to do: Update all DEB and RPM packaged installations of Zammad to the latest patched version to resolve the privilege escalation risk.

Why this is here: Known exploited -- CISA lists this as known-exploited in Zammad GmbH Zammad on 2026-10-02, so it is being used against people now -- that outranks severity. CRITICAL (CVSS 9.4); reach unrated (no entry in the reach table).

4. KNOWN EXPLOITED -- CVE-2015-5477

KNOWN EXPLOITED HIGH CVSS 7.5 published 2015-07-29

KNOWN EXPLOITED. CISA lists this as known-exploited in ISC BIND on 2026-10-08, so it is being used against people now -- that outranks severity.

What happened: A vulnerability in ISC BIND versions before 9.9.7-P2 and 9.10.2-P3 allows remote attackers to trigger a REQUIRE assertion failure. This causes the named daemon to exit, resulting in a denial of service.

Who is affected: Administrators running ISC BIND 9.x prior to 9.9.7-P2 or 9.10.x prior to 9.10.2-P3 are affected.

How serious: The issue is rated HIGH severity with a CVSS score of 7.5 due to its potential to disrupt DNS services.

What to do: Update ISC BIND to version 9.9.7-P2 or later, or 9.10.2-P3 or later, to resolve the denial of service risk.

Why this is here: Known exploited -- CISA lists this as known-exploited in ISC BIND on 2026-10-08, so it is being used against people now -- that outranks severity. HIGH (CVSS 7.5); reach 70/100 via "bind".
